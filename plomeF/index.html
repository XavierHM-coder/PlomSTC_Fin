02 / PROGRAM What you will learn

Practical skills for residential and commercial plumbing.

Build a working foundation in the tools, materials, systems, and safety procedures used throughout the plumbing trade.

01
Tools, materials, and safety Use common plumbing tools and materials while following essential job-site safety practices.
02
Pipe systems and joining methods Study fittings, pipe layouts, and common connection techniques.
03
Water supply and drainage Understand how water moves into, through, and out of a building.
04
Codes and regulations Learn the standards and regulations that guide safe plumbing work.
05
Fixtures, blueprints, and layouts Practice fixture installation and learn to read basic system drawings.
06
Troubleshooting and maintenance Develop a practical process for identifying and correcting common issues.
